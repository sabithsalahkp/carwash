Do I need an appointment?

No appointment necessary — walk-ins are welcome. You can also call (813) 755-2539 before visiting.

Where are you located?

Find us at 5018 N 22nd St, Tampa, FL 33610. You can use the Get Directions button below to open Google Maps.

How much does detailing cost?

Exterior hand washes start at $29.99. Standard Details start at $60, $70 or $80 depending on vehicle size. Premium Details start at $120, $150 or $180. Additional services may cost extra.

What's the difference between Standard and Premium?

Standard takes care of the essential interior and exterior cleaning. Premium goes further with paint decontamination, wax/sealant, enhanced interior cleaning and surface protection.

Can I add extra services?

Yes — headlight restoration, engine bay cleaning and pet hair removal are available as add-ons. Excessive dirt or grime is quoted on inspection.
